ENGINEERING NOTES

How the model works

01

Solar tracking

The reference sun angle is compared with the measured panel angle. A proportional tracking controller commands the motor to reduce the angle error.

eθ = θref − θpanel
02

PV generation

Effective irradiance depends on cloud cover and the angle between the sun and the panel. PV power is estimated from rated power and effective irradiance.

Geff = G × max(cos(Δθ), 0)
03

Voltage stabilization

A PI controller compares output voltage with its reference and adjusts the Buck converter duty ratio to regulate the DC output.

eV = Vref − Vout
04

Feedback signals

Panel position and output voltage are measured and returned to their respective summing junctions, forming two closed-loop control systems.

Feedback control
Model scope & limitations

This is an educational averaged model, not a hardware-validated design. The PV array is represented by a simplified power estimate at nominal PV voltage; it does not include a detailed single-diode I–V model or MPPT. The Buck converter is averaged (not individual MOSFET switching). A Buck converter can only step voltage down, so the reference voltage should remain below the PV nominal voltage (18 V). Motor and gate drivers are represented functionally.
